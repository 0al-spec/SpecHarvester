# P55-T10G1 Outcome-Level Purpose Anchors

## Objective

Add deterministic, source-bound outcome-level purpose anchors so provider output
and independent quality diagnostics distinguish a concrete user outcome from
package identity, discovery, import, repository, framework, and implementation
mechanics.

## Deliverables

- A provider-neutral purpose-anchor record derived only from digest-bound semantic
  product profile and bounded source documentation.
- Input-pack and provider-request projection of the anchor record as untrusted,
  evidence-only guidance.
- Deterministic purpose-specificity diagnostics for missing outcome support and
  mechanics-only purpose claims.
- Tests covering deterministic bindings, stale records, provider projection,
  eligible outcomes, review cases, and mechanics-only rejection.
- Validation, archive, review, and handoff evidence required by FLOW.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every anchor phrase identifies its source path and SHA-256 binding and the
  complete record has a deterministic digest.
- Outcome terms exclude candidate identity, repository identity, technology,
  package-boundary, discovery, import, and implementation-shape vocabulary.
- The provider receives anchors without gaining authority and without changing
  attempt, repair, evidence, or output budgets.
- Quality evaluation requires review when no purpose overlaps a supported outcome
  anchor and rejects a purpose made only from mechanics.
- Existing P55-T5 thresholds, proposal-only boundaries, and publication controls
  remain unchanged.
- Full configured quality gates pass with coverage at or above 90 percent.

## Dependencies

- P55-T10G immutable ten-repository calibration evidence.
- P55-T10E semantic product profile and digest-bound source projection.
- P55-T10F provider-neutral semantic quality and intent-routing diagnostics.

## Non-Goals

- Provider execution or corpus reruns.
- Semantic repair changes reserved for P55-T10G2.
- Acceptance, materialization, canonicalization, registry mutation, or publication.
